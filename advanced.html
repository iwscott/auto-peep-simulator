Physiology and limitations. This passive, single-compartment model uses an expiratory time constant τ = resistance × compliance, constant inspiratory flow and exponential passive expiration. At end expiration, estimated intrinsic PEEP = retained volume ÷ compliance. During an ideal end-expiratory occlusion, airway pressure equilibrates with alveolar pressure, so total PEEP ≈ set PEEP + intrinsic PEEP. The displayed pressure during ordinary unoccluded expiration is set PEEP, not alveolar pressure. Real patients have heterogeneous lung units, airway closure, active respiratory effort and other factors not represented here. Use clinical judgement and actual ventilator measurements.
